Do You Have Rajyog in Your Date of Birth? Know the Rare 4-5-6 Golden Yoga

Published Thu Sep 24 2026
Last Updated Sun Sep 27 2026
Read Time 3-4 minutes Minutes

What if three numbers in your date of birth form a combination that appears in only a small percentage of birth charts?

In the 8 Planes Lo Shu Grid, the combination of 4, 5, and 6 is known as Golden Yog or Rajyog. According to Dr. JC Chaudhry's published work on the 8 Planes Lo Shu Grid, this combination is found in approximately 2% to 3% of birth charts.

That makes Rajyog one of the most intriguing combinations to look for when studying a Lo Shu Grid.

But what does this combination actually represent?

What Is Rajyog in Numerology?

Rajyog, also called Golden Yog, refers to the combination of 4, 5, and 6 within the 8-plane Lo Shu Grid.

The significance comes from the relationship between these numbers rather than from any one number on its own.

4 + 5 + 6 = Golden Yog / Rajyog

Each number has its own significance in numerology.

Number 4 is associated with wealth, organisation, research, depth and systematic thinking.

Number 5 is connected with communication, balance, stability and understanding.

Number 6 is associated with luxury, wealth, creativity, social connections and material comforts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Rajyog in numerology?

Rajyog, also known as Golden Yog, is a combination of 4, 5, and 6 in the 8 Planes Lo Shu Grid. In numerology, this combination is associated with themes such as material growth, professional development, organisation, communication, creativity and achievement. Its interpretation should be considered within the complete Lo Shu Grid rather than in isolation.

How do I know if I have Rajyog in my Lo Shu Grid?

To find Rajyog, create your Lo Shu Grid using your date of birth and examine the relevant 8 Planes pattern. If the numbers 4, 5 and 6 form the required combination, it is referred to as Golden Yog or Rajyog. The complete grid should then be analysed to understand how this combination fits with other numbers and patterns.

What does 4-5-6 Golden Yog mean in numerology?

The 4-5-6 Golden Yog combines the qualities associated with Number 4, Number 5 and Number 6. It is traditionally examined in relation to themes such as wealth, career growth, communication, creativity, recognition and material comforts. However, having this combination does not by itself determine a person's success; its significance depends on the complete Lo Shu Grid.
